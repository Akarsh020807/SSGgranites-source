# Granite Gateway

Create a modern, elegant business website for "SSG GRANITES," a granite processing and trading enterprise backed by its own captive mining operations. The company sources Black Galaxy granite rough blocks from its own mine and converts them into high-quality cut and polished slabs for domestic and international markets. It also supplies Steel Grey and Black Pearl granite, which belong to the same broader granite family and complement the primary Black Galaxy offering. Take inspiration from the layout and tone of natural-stone supplier websites like aakargranite.com and archeanmarbles.com — clean, premium, image-forward, with a trustworthy industrial-craftsmanship feel — but tailor the content to reflect an integrated quarry-to-market operation, not a general multi-brand retailer.

COLOR PALETTE (use consistently across both pages):

- Primary accent: #A99171 (warm taupe/stone brown) — buttons, section highlights, hover states, icons

- Black (#1A1A1A or true black) — headings, nav text, footer background

- White (#FFFFFF) — main background, negative space

- Light grey (#F2F2F0 or similar) — alternating section backgrounds and card backgrounds

- Brown accents (deeper brown like #6B4E33) — secondary details, borders, dividers

Overall feel: warm, stone-inspired, minimal, premium — not flashy or colorful.

SITE STRUCTURE: Two connected pages with a shared header/navigation and footer.

- Home

- Products

=== HEADER / NAVIGATION (all pages) ===

- Logo/company name "SSG GRANITES" on the left

- Nav links: Home | Products | Contact Us (scrolls to contact section on Home)

- Sticky header, white background, black text, #A99171 accent on hover/active link

- A phone number or "Get a Quote" button on the right in the accent color

=== HOME PAGE ===

1. Hero Section

   - Full-width banner image of polished black granite slabs (Black Galaxy style — dark stone with golden/silver flecks)

   - Overlay headline: "SSG GRANITES" with tagline: "From Our Mine to Your Market — Premium Black Galaxy Granite"

   - Short subheading referencing integrated mining-to-export operations

   - CTA button ("Explore Our Granite" → links to Products page) in #A99171

2. About Us

   - Section titled "Who We Are"

   - Use this content (rephrase naturally, don't just paste as one block): SSG Granites is a granite processing and trading enterprise backed by its own mining operations. The company primarily sources Black Galaxy granite rough blocks from its captive mine and focuses on converting them into high-quality cut and polished slabs for sale in domestic and international markets. In addition to Black Galaxy, it also supplies Steel Grey and Black Pearl granite, which belong to the same broader family and complement its primary product offering. Its operations span raw-block sourcing, processing, cutting, polishing, finishing, quality selection, domestic distribution, and exports — an integrated quarry-to-market business model with greater control over raw-material sourcing, processing quality, and product consistency.

   - Supporting image: quarry/mine or slab-cutting facility

   - Row of 3-4 stat/feature cards, e.g.: "Captive Mining Operations," "3 Premium Granite Varieties," "Domestic & Export Reach," "Integrated Quarry-to-Market Model" — with icons in the accent color

3. Our Process (quarry-to-market flow)

   - Section titled "Our Integrated Process" or "From Mine to Market"

   - A horizontal step flow (numbered icons/cards) showing: Mining & Block Sourcing → Cutting & Processing → Polishing & Finishing → Quality Selection → Domestic Distribution & Export

   - Light grey background, short one-line description under each step

4. Our Granite (brief showcase on Home, full detail on Products page)

   - Section titled "Our Granite Range"

   - 3 cards: Black Galaxy (flagship — larger/featured card), Steel Grey, Black Pearl

   - Each card: image, name, one-line description, "View Details" link to Products page

5. Gallery / Facility & Finished Slabs

   - Responsive image grid or carousel: quarry/mine shots, block-cutting, polished slab close-ups, loaded shipments/exports

   - Subtle hover zoom effect

6. Why Choose Us

   - Row of 3-4 trust points: Own Mining = Consistent Supply, Strict Quality Selection, Export-Ready Processing, Competitive Direct-from-Source Pricing — with simple icons

7. Contact Us (bottom of Home page)

   - Section titled "Contact Us," dark (black or deep brown) background, white/accent text

   - Form fields: Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Comments/Message (textarea)

   - Submit button in #A99171, with a success message on submit (no backend needed unless specified)

   - Beside/below the form: company contact details (phone, email, address) and a placeholder map or location icon

   - Mention both domestic inquiries and export/bulk order inquiries are welcome

=== PRODUCTS PAGE ===

- Page header/banner: "Our Products" with intro line: "Premium Black Galaxy Granite and complementary varieties, processed in-house from block to polished slab."

- Feature Black Galaxy prominently at the top as the flagship product (larger hero card or dedicated section):

  - Image, description covering its deep black base with natural mineral flecks, durability, and popularity for countertops/flooring/cladding

  - Note that it's sourced directly from the company's own mine

- Below that, a grid/section for the two companion varieties:

  - Steel Grey Granite — image, description (grey tones, versatile for modern interiors/exteriors)

  - Black Pearl Granite — image, description (dark stone with distinct pearl-like flecks/patterns)

- For each of the 3 products, include: available finishes (polished, honed, leather, flamed), typical applications (countertops, flooring, cladding, monuments), and an "Enquire Now" button linking back to the Contact Us section on Home

- Optional: a short note/banner about custom sizing, bulk/export orders, and slab vs. tile availability

- Consistent card styling: white background, light grey border, #A99171 accent for labels/tags

=== FOOTER (all pages) ===

- Dark background (black or deep brown)

- Left: company name/logo, one-line description ("Integrated granite mining, processing & export")

- Middle: quick links (Home, Products, Contact)

- Right: contact details (phone, email, address) and social icons

- Bottom bar: "© 2026 SSG Granites. All rights reserved." in small grey text

=== GENERAL DESIGN NOTES ===

- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)

- Clean serif or semi-serif font for headings, simple sans-serif for body text

- Generous white space, no clutter

- Smooth scroll and subtle fade-in animations on scroll

- Use royalty-free stock images of black/dark granite slabs, quarry/mining sites, and stone-processing facilities as placeholders throughout — avoid marble or light-colored stone imagery since the product focus is dark granite
